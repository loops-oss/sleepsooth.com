Frequently asked questions

How many hours of sleep do adults need?

Adults should regularly get 7 or more hours of sleep a night, according to the joint consensus of the American Academy of Sleep Medicine and the Sleep Research Society.

What sounds help you fall asleep?

Steady sounds such as rain, ocean waves, wind or pink and brown noise help many people by masking sudden noises. Evidence is still limited, so pick whichever you find easiest to ignore and keep the volume low.

What is the fastest way to relax before bed?

Dim the lights, put the phone away and do a few minutes of slow breathing with a longer exhale than inhale. Writing tomorrow's to-do list before bed can also help you fall asleep faster.
