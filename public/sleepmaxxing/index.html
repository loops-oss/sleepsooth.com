Sleepmaxxing: what actually works, and what to skip

Sleepmaxxing (also spelled sleep maxxing or sleep maxing) is the social media trend of stacking habits, gadgets and supplements to get the best possible sleep. Some of it is solid. Some of it is theatre.

Sleepmaxxing works when it focuses on the basics: a consistent sleep and wake time, 7 or more hours in bed asleep, a dark, cool and quiet bedroom, morning daylight, and no caffeine in the afternoon. Trendy extras such as mouth taping, sleep cocktails and chasing a perfect sleep score have weak evidence, and some carry risks.

What is sleepmaxxing?

The word borrows the "-maxxing" suffix from online self-improvement culture, meaning to push something to its maximum. Sleepmaxxing videos show elaborate evening routines: magnesium drinks, mouth tape, blackout masks, white noise, cold rooms and sleep trackers. The goal of better sleep is a good one. The trouble is that the routines mix well-supported habits with untested hacks, and present them as equally important.

The evidence, habit by habit

HabitEvidenceVerdict
Same sleep and wake time every dayStrongDo it. Regularity is one of the most reliable ways to improve sleep.
7+ hours of sleep for adultsStrong (Watson et al., 2015, Sleep)The foundation. No hack replaces enough time asleep.
No caffeine within about 6 hours of bedGood (Drake et al., 2013)Do it. Caffeine taken 6 hours before bed still cut sleep by more than an hour in one lab study.
Dark, cool, quiet bedroomGoodDo it. Blackout curtains, a cooler room and steady background sound if your area is noisy.
Morning daylight and daytime activityGoodDo it. Light early in the day helps set your body clock.
CBT-I for ongoing insomniaStrong (Edinger et al., 2021, AASM guideline)The first-line treatment for chronic insomnia, ahead of sleeping pills.
White noise, rain or nature soundsLimited (Riedy et al., 2021, Sleep Medicine Reviews)Optional. Helpful for masking noise; keep it quiet.
MagnesiumMixedOptional. Small, inconsistent trials; most plausible if you are low in magnesium.
MelatoninSituationalUseful for jet lag and some body clock problems. Doses vary between products, so ask a pharmacist.
Sleep trackers and sleep scoresWeak for improving sleepUse lightly. Chasing a perfect score can create the anxiety that keeps you awake (Baron et al., 2017).
Mouth tapingWeak, with risks (2025 systematic review in PLOS One)Skip it unless a doctor suggests it. It can be dangerous with nasal blockage or sleep apnea.
"Sleepy girl" mocktails, tart cherryWeakHarmless for most people, but do not expect much.

A simple sleepmaxxing routine

  1. Fix your wake time, including weekends. Count back 7.5 to 9 hours for your bedtime.
  2. Get outside in the morning for 10 to 30 minutes of daylight.
  3. Stop caffeine by early afternoon and keep alcohol away from bedtime; it fragments sleep later in the night.
  4. Wind down for 20 to 30 minutes: dim lights, screens away, a relaxation routine.
  5. Make the room dark, cool and quiet. If noise wakes you, use steady sound such as rain or ocean waves at a low volume.
  6. If you cannot sleep after about 20 minutes, get up, do something calm in low light, and go back when sleepy.

When sleepmaxxing goes too far

Researchers use the word orthosomnia for an unhealthy preoccupation with perfect sleep, often driven by tracker data. If your routine is making you more anxious about bedtime, simplify it. Good sleep usually comes from doing a few boring things consistently, not from doing everything.

When to see a doctor

Talk to a doctor if you snore loudly and wake unrefreshed, someone notices you stop breathing at night, you fall asleep during the day without meaning to, or poor sleep lasts more than three months. These can be signs of sleep apnea or insomnia, which are treatable.

Frequently asked questions

What does sleepmaxxing mean?

Sleepmaxxing means optimising your sleep as much as possible with habits, products and routines. It became popular on TikTok and other social media.

Is sleep maxing the same as sleepmaxxing?

Yes. Sleep maxing, sleep maxxing and sleepmaxxing are different spellings of the same trend.

Does mouth taping help you sleep?

There is little good evidence that it does, and a 2025 systematic review warned it may be risky for people with nasal obstruction or sleep apnea. Skip it unless a doctor recommends it.

What is the most important sleepmaxxing habit?

Keeping the same wake time every day and giving yourself at least 7 hours of sleep. Everything else builds on those two.
